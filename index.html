Before you board

What happens at the launch?

We reveal the dates of Milky Way, Masters' Union's first-ever UG fest, and walk you through the vision. Be in the room when the dates drop.

Who can RSVP?

Masters' Union UG students, with your @mastersunion.org email. You can bring friends from MU on your RSVP.

How many seats are there?

300, and friends count as seats. The counter at the top is live. When it reaches zero you can still RSVP to join the waitlist, and we fill seats from it in order.

Where and when?

Friday 16 October, Auditorium, 8th floor, CDS Tower. Doors open at 2:45 PM and the evening runs until 9:30 PM.

Can I change my RSVP?

Yes. Tap "Edit my RSVP" on your ticket, or RSVP again with the same email. Your old entry is replaced, never doubled.

Something else?

Write to milkyway@mastersunion.org and a real human from the team will answer.
